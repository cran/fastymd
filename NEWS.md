# fastymd 0.2.0

- Added function `get_yday()` for obtaining the day of the year (1 to 366).

- Added function `get_wday()` for obtaining the day of the week (0 to 6,
  Sunday to Saturday). The numbering may change prior to release.
  
- Added functions `get_yearweekday()`, `get_yearweekday_week()` and 
  `get_yearweekday_year()` for obtaining year-week-day representations of 
  Date objects.

- Added `get_isoweek()` and `get_isoyear()` functions.

- Added `get_epiweek()` and `get_epiyear()` functions.
